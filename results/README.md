## Project Overview

This folder contains CSV files generated by the R script, providing numerical summaries of the protein sequence and its potential N-glycosylation motifs.

### 1. Protein Summary

File: SMPD1_summary.csv

Contains the total protein length and the number of potential N-glycosylation motifs identified in the sequence.

### 2. Amino Acid Counts

File: SMPD1_amino_acid_counts.csv

Provides the count and percentage of each amino acid in the protein sequence. These values support the amino acid composition figure.

### 3. Motif Positions

File: SMPD1_motif_positions.csv

Lists the positions and sequences of potential N-glycosylation motifs matching the pattern N-X-S/T, where X can be any amino acid except proline. These results support the motif-position figure but do not confirm actual glycosylation.

## Conclusion

The CSV files provide detailed numerical results that complement the visualizations. Together, they summarize the protein length, amino acid distribution, and locations of potential N-glycosylation motifs identified during the sequence analysis.
